Welcome to Manali

Manali is a beautiful hill station located in Himachal Pradesh. It is famous for mountains, rivers, valleys and pleasant weather.

Beautiful Nature

Snow-covered mountains, green forests and peaceful valleys make Manali a perfect destination for nature lovers.

Popular Places

Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass, Hidimba Temple and Mall Road are some of the most popular places to visit in Manali.

Why Visit Manali?

Manali is ideal for adventure, photography, sightseeing and enjoying the peaceful beauty of the mountains.

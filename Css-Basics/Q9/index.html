Sunday Edition | August 30, 2026

Technology is changing the world at a rapid pace. New innovations are helping people communicate, work and learn more efficiently.

Artificial intelligence has become one of the most important technologies of the modern world. It is being used in healthcare, education, business and entertainment.

Modern cities are also becoming smarter. Smart systems can improve transportation, energy consumption and public services.

Education has changed significantly because of digital technologies. Students can now access educational resources from almost anywhere.

The future will continue to bring new technologies. People and organizations must adapt to these changes to make the best use of modern innovations.

Digital communication has also changed the way people interact with each other. Information can now be shared instantly across the world.
